Privacy Policy

Casa Contas · Last updated: October 9, 2026

Casa Contas (“the App”) helps you track personal and household bills. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

1. Who is responsible

The App is operated by Alice Beatriz Arnaud Mota (alice.arnaud.mota@gmail.com).

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, password (stored securely by our auth provider), and display name.
  • Household and bill data: houses you create or join, members, categories, bill amounts, due dates, payment status, payment methods, optional bank labels you enter, reminders, and related notes or documents you upload.
  • Device data: local preferences (such as selected house) stored on your device, and notification permissions if you enable reminders.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide bill tracking, calendars, shared household features, and reminders
  • To process optional bill document scanning / parsing features you choose to use
  • To maintain security and fix issues

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Service providers

We use third-party infrastructure to run the App, including:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • Google Gemini — optional AI parsing when you upload a bill document
  • Apple — App Store distribution and push notification delivery

These providers process data only as needed to provide their services under their own privacy policies.

5. Sharing within a household

If you join or create a household, other members of that household can see shared household bills and related information for that house. Personal data you keep private in the App (where the App provides that separation) is not shown to other members.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and bill data while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email above.

7. Security

We use industry-standard protections such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and authenticated access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us to make a request.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: alice.arnaud.mota@gmail.com
